# V3.1 · J1 版本更新 / J1 Update

2026-10-11 · V3 → V3.1 · 基于队友的 `training-platform-v3.zip` / Based on the team's v3

## 中文版

- **职涯 AI 助理：**接入 AI 对话，提供个性化职涯建议、路线比较、技能差距分析及具体行动建议，帮助用户明确方向和下一步。
- **学历资料：**新增可选的学历程度、课程名称及已完成／修读中状态，让职涯建议和初次 CV 草稿更贴合用户背景。
- **问卷优化：**新增自动保存、继续作答及更清晰的同分说明，减少重复填写，让结果更容易理解。
- **中英文切换：**切换语言时保留聊天记录和所选路线，路线说明即时切换，用户无需重新开始对话。

沿用原有设计，重点完善毕业生路线的使用体验。队友已有的问卷、五年规划、专业证书、面试、评分和创业者功能均保留。

## English

- **Career AI assistant:** Integrated AI conversations for personalised career advice, path comparisons, skill-gap analysis and practical next steps, helping users decide what to pursue and how to start.
- **Education details:** Added optional education level, programme and completed/in-progress status, making career advice and the initial CV draft more relevant to the user's background.
- **Questionnaire improvements:** Added autosave/resume and clearer explanations for tied scores, reducing repeated work and making results easier to understand.
- **Chinese/English switching:** Language switching preserves chat history and the selected path, with instant bilingual path explanations, so users can continue without restarting.

The original design is retained, with improvements focused on the graduate route. The team's existing questionnaire, five-year plan, professional qualifications, interview, scoring and founder features are retained.
